
# LAST MESSAGE Cyberbullying Investigation Game

## Pitch  
A narrative investigation game for teenagers (12–16 yo) that explores cyberbullying through fragmented digital conversations, conflicting perspectives, and social interactions, combining the chronological reconstruction puzzles of No Case Should Remain Unsolved with the psychological dialogue system of Disco Elysium.

Players reconstruct events by analyzing screenshots, organizing conversations, and identifying relationships between characters, while an internal dialogue system representing empathy, suspicion, logic, and social pressure challenges their assumptions and encourages them to distinguish between facts, interpretations, and personal judgments.

Through an interactive social network that reveals how messages, reactions, and shared content spread between individuals, the idea is to beyond identifying who is responsible and encourages players to understand the complexity of online relationships, recognize the consequences of their actions, and develop critical thinking, empathy, and digital responsibility.

## Concept Inspirations 
- https://www.stoplaviolence.net (serious game about investigation quests about victims of rumors and false information spread in middle school)
- https://www.mycyberstory.fr (users are placed in realistic cyberattack scenario, phishing, email compromise, and crisis management and make strategic live decisions to advance the story)


## Game Mechanic Inspirations 
### No Case Should Remain Unsolved
- Chronology Reconstruction (re-ogarnise dialogues, who’s speaking)
- Keywords, hashtags associations (memory stimulation, bring new memories)
- Key System (resolve correct sequences and questions to gain keys, it unlock major clues)
![image4](/images/images-29.jpeg)
 
### Disco Elysium
- Text-based RPG (action unfolds through dialogues, exploration, and the detective's internal thoughts)
- Living skills (skills speak directly to your mind to advise you, deceive you, or analyze situations)
- Thought Cabinet (mental inventory to internalize philosophical ideas that alter your dialogue choices)
- Health and Morale (Two vital life points, physical and psychological) drops to zero, it's game over
- Dice-based skill checks
